      If you're reading this, you already did the hardest part. You started.

      I made this book because I keep meeting brilliant people who stay invisible at work. Not because they lack talent, but because nobody can see it yet.

      So here are three things I'd love you to keep:

      1. Your normal work is worth sharing. Even the small stuff.
      2. Nobody is waiting for perfect. They're waiting for useful.
      3. Thirty small days will change more than one big plan.

      When your first post goes live, pin it on the cheer wall. I want to be the first one cheering.

      With belief in you,
